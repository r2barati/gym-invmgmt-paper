gym-invmgmt paper and reproducibility artifacts

Benchmark code, scenarios, trained checkpoints, result tables, and figure-generation scripts for the arXiv paper on auditable inventory-management policy evaluation.

Paper

gym-invmgmt: An Open Benchmarking Framework for Inventory Management Methods

Reza Barati and Qinmin Vivian Hu. Submitted May 12, 2026. arXiv:2605.11355 [cs.LG]. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2605.11355.

The paper compares optimization, heuristic, imitation-learning, reinforcement-learning, graph-based, transformer-based, residual, LLM-diagnostic, and MARL-mode controllers under a shared environment and KPI contract.

Quick Reproduction

Run the benchmark matrix and regenerate tables and figures from the checked-in scripts.

bash download_weights.sh
cd benchmarks
python3 run_benchmarks.py --agent ALL --seeds 10
python3 ../paper/figures/extract_appendix_tables.py
python3 ../paper/figures/regenerate_appendix_figures.py
Cite as: Reza Barati and Qinmin Vivian Hu, gym-invmgmt: An Open Benchmarking Framework for Inventory Management Methods, arXiv:2605.11355, 2026.
